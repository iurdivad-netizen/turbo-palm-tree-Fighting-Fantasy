These results describe this build and these bots, so read them as a diagnosis, not a final balance verdict.

9 · Findings

✓ Fixed: all seven pit traps now have a Luck save done

Sections 245 and 189 (earlier) and now 7, 167, 275, 150 and 107: the instant-death band of each roll asks for a Luck test to catch the edge. The injury is one notch worse than the neighbouring survivable band (8, 6, 4, 4 and 7 STAMINA), and only a failed test falls to the death page. Deaths at the five new ones: 753 → 486; at 245 and 189 the earlier fix cut them from 1,118 to 466.

✓ Fixed earlier: preparation rooms and the 362 funnel done

The nine preparation rooms return to their hub and 395 lets an unprepared player back away. Entries into the 362 ambush: 2,441 → 1,049 → 1,306.

1 LUCK is a draining resource that the saves depend on high

The book now asks for 40 Luck tests plus 8 more nested inside dice rolls (the pit saves and one slip), and each costs 1 LUCK. Only 24 sections give LUCK back (shrines, fountains, discoveries and potions). Simulated players reach the saves with an average LUCK of 7.3. At LUCK 7 a Luck test passes about 58% of the time, at 6 about 42%, at 3 about 8%. That is why the traps still kill: roughly one player in ten at 245 and 189 (they are rolled first, with LUCK still high), and about 25% of rolls at section 7, which is rolled again on every visit and tends to come later.

2 The Widow is the final wall high

The informed player wins the final fight 51% of the time. She is immune to non-magical weapons and her Wail costs 2 SKILL for the whole fight on an unlucky roll, so a player who reaches her without a magical weapon cannot win; the book does not guarantee one (the informed bot goes and fetches one). Consider placing a magical weapon on the main route, or letting the silver weapons count.

3 The "incomplete victory" ending does not exist medium

Section 362 ends in the text with "THE END (Partial Victory)", but in the data its fight leads on to section 363, an ordinary Phantom Servant ambush, and then back into the hub at 335. Players who survive the three Shadows and the weakened Widow get no ending. Add a proper ending section (or make 362 an ending after the fight).

4 Wrong "ending" flags on 9 mid-game sections medium

Sections 14, 24, 29, 36, 38, 41, 56, 152, 297 are flagged as endings although they carry choices, dice rolls or combat. The reader prints a "Victory!" or "The End" banner on them while the story continues (24 and 56 are the Mayor refusing to help, flagged as victory). This came from the converter reading "(VICTORY!)" or "THE END" in prose. Remove isEnding from these sections.

5 17 sections can never be reached in play medium

Section 180 lists 19 places to explore after the fight, but its victory link jumps straight to 259, so the menu is never shown. Sections 260, 261, 262, 263, 264, 265, 268, 269, 270, 271, 273, 274, 276, 277, 278, 279, 280 have no other entrance. These are the 17 sections the bots never entered in 3,000 games. Fix: remove onVictorySection from the fight so its menu appears after the win (and keep the jump target as one of the menu items).

6 Some Luck-test failures lead somewhere that does not fit medium

The conversion follows the book's own cross-references literally, and some of them do not match the story: failing the test in 138, 224, 256, 295 sends you to 97 (a healing shrine that fully restores STAMINA and gives +1 LUCK); failing the test in 142, 304 sends you to 117 (a journal with the curse timeline); failing the test in 228 sends you to 152 (a study with a journal). In those sections an unlucky roll is a reward. I did not guess the author's intent; they need a human decision (most likely a real trap section, such as the dart trap in 58).

10 · Method and limits

  • Engine: the bots drive the real index.html game logic (GameState, fightRound, handleChoice, Luck tests, dice rolls, item use, conditions) in headless Chromium, with delays and DOM rendering switched off.
  • Randomness: a seeded generator (seed 20240601) feeds every die and every bot decision, so the run is reproducible. 1,000 games per policy, 3,000 in total, in 9s. All four builds use the same seed. A change of about 2.6 points in a win rate is within run-to-run noise.
  • Shared bot habits: flee when STAMINA ≤ 5 and the fight allows it; eat or drink healing items when STAMINA falls below 45% of the maximum (and below 60% before a fight); a game ends after 3,000 moves.
  • Roll once: encounter hubs (155, 221, 301, 335, 355, 127) and the pit traps 150, 189, 245 and 275 roll only on the first visit per game. Sections 7, 107 and 167 and the other traps roll again on every visit.
  • Limits: bots are not people. Random under-plays (never plans) and the informed bot over-plays (has read the book), so treat them as bounds. The explorer retreats from the final door when it can. Gold is never spent (the book has no shop). Reading text, noticing hints and risk-aversion are not modelled.
