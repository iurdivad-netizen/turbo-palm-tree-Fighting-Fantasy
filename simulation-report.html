Both structural problems are fixed, and the win rates moved a long way. Informed player 87.5% → 59.9% → 77.2% across the three builds; careful explorer 1.9% → 3.0% → 13.3%; random walker 1.4% → 1.2% → 5.8%. (Original build → chance gates converted → structural fixes, same seed and bots.)

  • Pit traps 245 and 189. A roll of 1–2 is now a Luck test instead of death. Deaths there fell from 1,118 to 418 across the 3,000 games.
  • One-way doors. The preparation rooms return to their hub and 395 lets an unprepared player back away. Entries into the deadly 362 ambush fell from 2,441 to 1,049.

What now limits the book is smaller and more ordinary: the remaining instant-death rolls (section 7 and 167 at 50%, 275, 150 and 107 at 33%) account for 753 deaths, and the Widow's final fight is lost 52% of the time by the informed player (122 of 235 fights). Random players win 5.8% of games, which is low but no longer the near-zero it was. These results describe this build and these bots, so read them as a diagnosis, not a final balance verdict.

9 · Findings

✓ Fixed: pit traps 245 and 189 done

A roll of 1–2 no longer sends the player to the death page. It now asks for a Luck test to catch the edge (the player loses 4 STAMINA at 245, 3 at 189) and only a failed test falls. At full LUCK that turns a one-in-three death into about one in eighteen. In the simulation the observed rate is 9.9% at 245 and 9.4% at 189 of the games that reached them, because players arrive with LUCK already spent on earlier tests (each test costs 1). Deaths at these two sections: 1,118 → 418.

✓ Fixed: the final-preparation rooms done

Rooms 377, 380, 381, 382, 383, 384, 385, 386, 387, 389, 390 now return to their hub (388, or the staircase 355 for 377 and 380) instead of carrying the player to 395, and the fights among them hand back to 388 after the win. Section 395 offers "back away and prepare further" to a player without both the Silver Key and her true name, and section 29 offers "keep searching". Entries into the 362 ambush: 2,441 → 1,049. Unprepared arrivals at 395 are still common (86% for the explorer) but most of them now back away.

2 The "incomplete victory" ending does not exist medium

Section 362 ends in the text with "THE END (Partial Victory)", but in the data its fight leads on to section 363, an ordinary Phantom Servant ambush, and then back into the hub at 335. Players who survive the three Shadows and the weakened Widow get no ending. Add a proper ending section (or make 362 an ending after the fight).

3 Wrong "ending" flags on 9 mid-game sections medium

Sections 14, 24, 29, 36, 38, 41, 56, 152, 297 are flagged as endings although they carry choices, dice rolls or combat. The reader prints a "Victory!" or "The End" banner on them while the story continues (24 and 56 are the Mayor refusing to help, flagged as victory). This came from the converter reading "(VICTORY!)" or "THE END" in prose. Remove isEnding from these sections.

4 17 sections can never be reached in play medium

Section 180 lists 19 places to explore after the fight, but its victory link jumps straight to 259, so the menu is never shown. Sections 260, 261, 262, 263, 264, 265, 268, 269, 270, 271, 273, 274, 276, 277, 278, 279, 280 have no other entrance. These are the 17 sections the bots never entered in 3,000 games. Fix: remove onVictorySection from the fight so its menu appears after the win (and keep the jump target as one of the menu items).

5 Some Luck-test failures lead somewhere that does not fit medium

The conversion follows the book's own cross-references literally, and some of them do not match the story: failing the test in 138, 224, 256, 295 sends you to 97 (a healing shrine that fully restores STAMINA and gives +1 LUCK); failing the test in 142, 304 sends you to 117 (a journal with the curse timeline); failing the test in 228 sends you to 152 (a study with a journal). In those sections an unlucky roll is a reward. I did not guess the author's intent; they need a human decision (most likely a real trap section, such as the dart trap in 58).

6 Still open note

22: section 22 is a Ghoul paralysis test whose SKILL penalty must carry into the next fight; it needs a carry-over penalty mechanic. The Widow's final fight (394) is now the informed player's main killer: 122 losses in 235 fights (48% won). She is immune to non-magical weapons and her Wail can cost 2 SKILL for the whole fight, so a player who reaches her without a magical weapon cannot win; the book does not guarantee one.

10 · Method and limits

  • Engine: the bots drive the real index.html game logic (GameState, fightRound, handleChoice, Luck tests, dice rolls, item use, conditions) in headless Chromium, with delays and DOM rendering switched off.
  • Randomness: a seeded generator (seed 20240601) feeds every die and every bot decision, so the run is reproducible. 1,000 games per policy, 3,000 in total, in 9s. All three builds use the same seed.
  • Shared bot habits: flee when STAMINA ≤ 5 and the fight allows it; eat or drink healing items when STAMINA falls below 45% of the maximum (and below 60% before a fight); a game ends after 3,000 moves.
  • Roll once: encounter hubs (155, 221, 301, 335, 355, 127) and the pit traps (150, 189, 245, 275) roll only on the first visit per game. Other traps roll again on every visit.
  • Limits: bots are not people. Random under-plays (never plans) and the informed bot over-plays (has read the book), so treat them as bounds. The explorer retreats from the final door when it can. Gold is never spent (the book has no shop). Reading text, noticing hints and risk-aversion are not modelled.
